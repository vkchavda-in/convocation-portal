[H4] Our Vision & Mission

[H2] GANPAT UNIVERSITY -A Destination Of Smart Education

[P] Ganpat University and the township of Ganpat Vidyanagar, a high-tech education campus is a joint initiative, purely philanthropic in nature, by a large number of industrialists and technocrats, noble farmers and affluent businessmen for the mission of “Social Upliftment through Education”.

[H4] What is Our VISION?

[P] ``The vision of GUNI AM CoE is to create and enable a sustainable ecosystem for product innovation in India with emphasis on educational programs, research activities, designing, and testing through collaborative efforts between academia institutes, industries, and government bodies using the Additive Manufacturing technology.``

[H2] We Provide Worldclass Platform For Additive Manufacturing Technology Research.

[H4] 24/7 Online support

[P] We are always available here to connect with the outer world regarding any type of research work and collaboration form Industry.

[H4] Smart Labs

[P] We are providing smart and digitalized testing Laboratories for all Research Works and always ready to update them as per Future requirements.
